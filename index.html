Privacy Policy for the TRACES App

Last updated: October 2026

The TRACES App (“TRACES,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is developed by the Arctic Centre at the University of Groningen. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you record using the app.

This Privacy Policy explains how the TRACES App handles information. TRACES is designed as an offline field-recording tool for recording GPS tracks, photographs, observations, and notes during field trips.

1. Information We Collect

The TRACES App allows you to record information that you choose to enter or create while using the app, including:

  • GPS location information used to record your trips and tracks.
  • Photographs taken using the app.
  • Descriptions, notes, and other observation information that you enter.
  • Information associated with recorded observations, such as categories, types, species, and quantities.

This information is stored locally on your device. TRACES does not automatically upload this information to servers operated by the University of Groningen or by the developers of the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information recorded in TRACES is used to provide the app's field-recording and trip-documentation functionality.

TRACES does not use your recorded information for advertising, behavioural tracking, or analytics.

3. Location Information

TRACES uses your device's location services when you choose to record a trip. Location information is used to create and display the GPS track associated with your field trip.

Location recording can be started and stopped by you. Location data recorded by the app is stored locally on your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it.

4. Photos and Camera

TRACES can use your device's camera when you choose to take photographs of field observations or sites.

Photographs taken through the app are stored locally on your device together with the associated observation or trip information.

5. Data Storage

TRACES is designed to store recorded trip and observation information locally on your device. The app does not require you to create an account and does not provide a developer-operated cloud storage service for your recorded data.

6. Exporting and Sharing

TRACES allows you to export or share information when you choose to do so. When you use an Android or iOS sharing function, the information is transferred using the method or service that you select.

Once you choose to share or transfer information to another person, application, service, or storage location, that information may be subject to the privacy practices of the receiving service.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your recorded data remains on your device until you choose to delete it or otherwise remove it from your device.

The developers of TRACES do not have access to locally stored trip, location, photograph, or observation data unless you independently choose to share or provide that information.

8. Third-Party Services

TRACES uses standard software libraries and platform services to provide functions such as mapping, location services, camera access, local data storage, and sharing.

TRACES does not intentionally send your recorded field data to third parties for advertising, analytics, or behavioural tracking.

9. Children's Privacy

TRACES is a field-research and documentation application and is not specifically directed toward children.

10. Security

Because recorded information is stored locally on your device, its security also depends on the security of your device and operating system. We recommend using the security features provided by your device, such as a passcode, password, or biometric authentication.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the TRACES App or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new revision date.
